# LunarForge Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

LunarForge is a free game launcher that allows users to browse, download, install, and play games developed by FNMCfire.

## 1. User Profiles and Game Data

LunarForge allows users to create local player profiles using usernames.

Profile settings, achievements, and game statistics are stored on the user's computer. LunarForge does not require users to create an online account.

## 2. Internet Connections and Downloads

LunarForge connects to online services, including Google Drive, to retrieve game information and download game files.

These services may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, according to their own privacy policies.

## 3. Data Sharing

LunarForge is not designed to upload player profiles, achievements, or game statistics to an online account.

## 4. Data Storage and Deletion

LunarForge stores its local settings and player information on the user's computer.

Users can manage their local data through available application features. Removing the application may not automatically delete all locally stored files.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when LunarForge's features or data practices change.

## 6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact FNMCfire through the official LunarForge support contact.
